# Draw on a canvas

Circle an idea, underline a box or scribble a note in the margin with the
**Draw** tool, then move or remove what you drew.

## Draw

Pick **Draw** (the pencil) in the toolbar, or press `D`, and drag across the
board. What you drew stays as a smooth stroke, with no arrowhead at either
end. A tap that does not travel leaves nothing behind.

Strokes you draw one straight after another, close together, become one
drawing: a handwritten word selects, moves and recolours as a whole.

## Move a drawing

Switch back to **Select** (`V`), then press the stroke and drag it. The whole
drawing travels; nothing about its shape changes. With a mouse or a finger
alike, dragging the stroke anywhere along its length moves it.

A selected drawing shows a square handle at each end. Dragging one moves that
end, and dropping it on a box attaches the end to the box. On a touch screen
each end takes a finger-sized press around the handle.

A drawing does not show grab points along its length: its shape is the path
your hand took, and reshaping it is drawing it again. A line ruled between two
points — one dragged out of a box's connect handle — keeps its round bend
handles, so its bends can still be placed by hand.
